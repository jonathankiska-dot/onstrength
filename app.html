Start small
One position, or one standing meeting. Both are on the list on the left, both take a minute, and neither needs a cost centre or a team built.
Or the whole team
Build your team once and every position is costed to the day — vacancies, step increases, part-time, people arriving and leaving — against the budget you were given.
Where the numbers come from
Salaries read from the published pay tables. Your own estimates are kept separate from them, and both are shown, so a figure can be checked rather than taken on trust.
