Onstrength Salary planning tool How to use it
Work out where your salary budget lands.
The problem
Forecasting salaries by hand means rebuilding a spreadsheet every month and chasing figures from a finance advisor with their own deadlines. It arrives late, in someone else's format, and rarely when you need to make the decision.
What it is
The same forecast, in your hands. Every position costed from the published pay tables, pay period by pay period — on demand, without asking anyone.
How it works
Budget, less base salary, less six estimates you set. What is left is your surplus or your overrun. Five steps, about five minutes.
Everything stays in this browser. No account, nothing uploaded, and nobody else — including me — can see what you enter.
