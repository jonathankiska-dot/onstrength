What it deliberately doesn't do
  • It doesn't track actuals. This plans the year; it doesn't reconcile it. That's a later job.
  • It doesn't count benefits or bonuses. They're charged centrally and never reach your budget.
  • It isn't a system of record. Check anything that matters against the ledger.

Why the numbers hold up

Precision should follow materiality. The 90% that can be computed exactly is computed exactly; the rest is your judgement, labelled as such.

Costed by pay period

107 classification groups, 720 levels, 3,454 published steps. A fiscal year is 26 periods of 14 days, and every position is costed that way — prorated by the day when someone starts on the 12th.

Sliced at every change

A step increment, a move to 80%, a period of unpaid leave, a departure in November — each one cuts the year, and each slice is costed at the rate and hours in force. Nothing is averaged.

Nothing leaves your device

No account, no server, no upload. Your roster lives in your own browser storage. Names are optional and stay local — which is why this link can be shared freely.

One tap

Your browser can install this directly.

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Tap the Share button — the box with an arrow coming out of the top, in the bar at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Scroll the sheet up, past the row of app icons, into the grey list of actions below.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Not in the list? Scroll that list to the very bottom and tap Edit Actions… — if "Add to Home Screen" is there with a green +, tap it and it'll come back. If it isn't there at all, your phone is managed by your employer and the option is switched off. That's not something you can change, and it doesn't matter — the link works exactly the same in the browser.

Reading this inside another app? Links opened from email, Teams or LinkedIn run in a cut-down browser with no install option. Tap ••• or Open in Safari first.
